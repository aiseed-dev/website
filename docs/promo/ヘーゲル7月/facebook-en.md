Over eleven days I wrote eight pieces: China's Kimi K3, the fifty-company open-weights letter NVIDIA hosted, Microsoft's earnings, and today's scheduled release of a near-frontier model's weights, for free.

After finishing, I noticed the eight pieces were not separate stories. Run them through a philosopher who died two hundred years ago and they parse as one sentence.

Hegel's master-slave dialectic: the master who pushes labor onto others becomes powerless; the one who touches the substance through labor gains the power. Nadella, who **bought** the frontier model, and Yang Zhilin, who **built** a 2.8-trillion-parameter one and opened it — that is the modern casting.

The same lens exposes the soft spot of the AI business: **to sell intelligence, you must expose its outputs. And exposed outputs always become teaching material.** The master's power is copied the moment it is exercised.

And the fifty signatures? The split fell along the P&L, not ideology. Hegel called this the cunning of reason — history runs on everyone's self-interest, and a restriction threat organized the largest open coalition in history within three days. Nobody had that as their goal.

The conclusion fits in one line: keep touching reality, and never hand over judgment.
