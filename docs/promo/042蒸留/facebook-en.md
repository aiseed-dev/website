Everyone is arguing about whether Kimi K3 was distilled from Claude. Almost nobody has noticed that the technique works outside AI.

Distillation: put questions to an expensive model, collect the answers, train a cheap model on the input-output pairs. The internals are not needed. The behavior is enough.

Now replace "model" with "business system."

A test is nothing but putting a question to a system and recording the answer. Enter an order, this document is raised; close the period, this balance appears. Write enough of that down and the behavior of the business is in your hands — and AI can build another implementation that does the same thing. Structurally, that is black-box distillation. And what you are copying is not the vendor's code. It is how your own business ought to behave — which was yours to begin with.

The consequence nobody prices in: outsourcing existed only in a middle layer of work that required expertise but not business judgment. Judgment moves toward the company. Work moves toward AI. The width in between approaches zero.
